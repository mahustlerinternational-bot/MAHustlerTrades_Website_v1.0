## Question 1
TYPE: single_choice
POINTS: 1
PROMPT: What is the primary purpose of a stop loss?
- [x] To limit the predefined risk if the trade moves against the position
- [ ] To guarantee that every trade closes in profit
- [ ] To increase the position size automatically
- [ ] To remove all market volatility
EXPLANATION: A stop loss helps enforce the predefined risk boundary. It cannot guarantee a profit or remove market volatility.

## Question 2
TYPE: multiple_choice
POINTS: 2
PROMPT: Which two controls directly support disciplined position risk?
- [x] Predetermined position sizing
- [x] A defined invalidation level
- [ ] Increasing risk after a loss
- [ ] Entering without a trading plan
EXPLANATION: Position sizing and a defined invalidation level establish the amount at risk before the trade is opened.

## Question 3
TYPE: true_false
POINTS: 1
PROMPT: A trader should increase risk immediately after a losing trade to recover the loss.
- [ ] True
- [x] False
EXPLANATION: Recovery trading increases emotional and account risk. Risk limits should remain consistent with the approved trading plan.
